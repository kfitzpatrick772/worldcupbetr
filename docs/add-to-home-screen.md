# 📲 Add the 2026 World Cup Bracket to your phone

Put the pool one tap away — it saves like an app, opens full-screen, and the
scores update on their own.

**The link:** https://friendlybet.up.railway.app

When it's installed you'll see this icon on your home screen:
**black square, "WC" in green, "2026" in white.**

---

## iPhone / iPad (Safari)

> Must be **Safari** — Chrome on iPhone can't do this.

1. Open **https://friendlybet.up.railway.app** in Safari.
2. Tap the **•••** (More) button.
3. Tap **Share** — the square with an arrow pointing up (⬆️).
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Tap **Add** (top-right).

Done — the **WC 2026** icon is now on your home screen.

*(Shortcut: on the leaderboard there's an **"📲 Add to Home Screen"** button that
shows these same steps.)*

---

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open **https://friendlybet.up.railway.app** in Chrome.
2. Tap the **"📲 Add to Home Screen"** button on the leaderboard
   **— or —** tap the **⋮** menu (top-right) and choose **Add to Home screen / Install app**.
3. Tap **Install / Add**.

Done — the **WC 2026** icon is now on your home screen.

---

## That's it

- Tap the icon anytime to jump straight to the live board.
- No login needed to view — just open and watch.
- Scores and the leaderboard refresh automatically.
